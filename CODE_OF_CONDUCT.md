# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of SentinAI pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or location.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Use clear, professional language in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Respect differing technical opinions and approaches

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Dismissing contributions without explanation
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or bad-faith issues

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any other official project channels.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to **er.masanja01@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and responded to promptly. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate these standards.
